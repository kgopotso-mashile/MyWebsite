### Part3 


**On every page**
- Adds the extra styling it needs, so your CSS stays untouched.
- Adds hidden business data for search engines: the salon name, phone number, address, opening hours and map position, for SEO.
- Highlights the current page in the navigation bar.
- Adds a shadow under the header once you scroll.
- Shows a back-to-top button after you scroll down.
- Fades each section in as it scrolls into view. This is switched off for visitors whose device asks for less motion.
- Opens any image you click in a lightbox. Esc or a click closes it. On the Services gallery you also get next and previous arrows (buttons or keyboard) and an "image 3 / 21" counter.

**Home page**
- Shows a badge saying "We are open now" or "Currently closed", based on your 08:00–19:30 hours in South African time.
- Adds "Make an Enquiry" and "View Services" buttons.

**Services page**
Adds a "Find Your Perfect Treatment" section built from your seven services.
Has All, Women and Men tabs to filter by category.
Has a live search box that highlights the matching words.
Has a sort menu: price low to high, high to low, or A to Z.
Shows a result count and a service card for each match.
Opens a pop-up with the details when you click a card. It has an "Enquire about this" button that takes the visitor to the enquiry page with that service already chosen, and a call button.

**Enquiry page** (handles both forms)
Validates the form as described under "Form checking" below.
Shows an estimated price and time under the dropdowns as the visitor picks.
Pre-selects the service when the visitor arrives from the Services page.
Checks that an uploaded design is an image under 5 MB.
After a valid submit, shows a response card with the service, estimated cost, time andavailability for the chosen date, plus a call button and a "Make another enquiry" button.

**Contact page**
Validates the contact form the same way.
After a valid submit, shows a summary and a "Send email" button that opens the visitor's email app, addressed to the recipient set in the code.
Adds a "Find Us" section with your address, an interactive Leaflet map with a pop-up marker, and a "Get directions" button for Google Maps. The script looks up the address on OpenStreetMap and falls back to saved coordinates if that fails. Scroll-zoom turns on only after the visitor clicks the map, so the page doesn't get stuck while scrolling.

**About, Enquiry and Contact pages**
Adds a "Frequently Asked Questions" accordion (hours, men's services, booking, prices, treatment length).

**Form checking, used on both forms**
Required fields and dropdowns that must be changed from "--SELECT--".
Names must be letters only, 2 to 50 characters.
Email addresses must look valid.
Phone numbers must be South African (for example 066 422 4639 or +27 66 422 4639).
Dates can't be in the past or more than a year ahead, and times must fall inside your opening hours.
Messages must be 10 to 500 characters, with a live character counter.
Error messages appear under each field, with red or green borders, and the form shakes if you submit with mistakes.
The submit button shows "Sending…" and is disabled while the form is sent.
The form is sent without reloading the page (AJAX). Until you set a `formEndpoint` in `CONFIG`, the sending is simulated, so nothing is stored anywhere.
If sending fails, a friendly message tells the visitor to try again or call.

**Things it does not do for real**
The availability message doesn't check a real booking calendar. It only repeats your opening hours and the date chosen.
The prices are placeholders inside your R130–R300 range until you replace them.
